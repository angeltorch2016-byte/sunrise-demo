About Sunrise Africa Properties

Sunrise Africa Properties Limited develops and manages residential and commercial real estate across Nigeria's fastest-growing corridors, serving high-net-worth individuals, diaspora investors and institutional partners who expect the same rigour they'd get from any other asset class.

Developer & asset manager

We develop, sell and continue to manage our estates post-handover — not a one-time agency transaction.

Strategic corridors

Positioned in Ibeju-Lekki, Eleko, Abuja and Ikorodu — corridors defined by infrastructure growth and diaspora demand.

Documentation-first

Governor's consent, approved survey plans and deeds of assignment are prepared and issued for every allocation.
